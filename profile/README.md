## Why Torqa

- 💸 **No subscription, no account.** Torqa has no server or tracking of its own, and riding never
  needs the internet. Your rides stay on your computer.
- 🗺️ **Ride any road.** Any GPX route becomes a stylized 3D world from real terrain and
  OpenStreetMap, or ride along a real video that plays at your speed.
- 🚴 **Your trainer, your gear.** Bluetooth FTMS smart trainers and heart-rate straps, with 24
  virtual gears shifted from the keyboard, Shimano Di2 hood buttons, a Zwift Click or your phone.
- 💪 **Train with a plan.** Workouts with an editor and FTP tests, ghosts and pacers, your power
  curve, fitness and records, FIT export and upload to intervals.icu and Strava.
- ⚖️ **Open all the way.** GPL-3.0, built in Rust and Godot 4.
